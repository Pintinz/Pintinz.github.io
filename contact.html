Contact Noetra Careers

The quickest way to reach us is Help & Support inside the app (Profile, then Help & Support). You can also email careeros.info@gmail.com.

Help with your account or the app

Use Help & Support in the app so we can see your app version and reply in the app. If you can't sign in, email careeros.info@gmail.com from the address on your account.

A listing is wrong, closed or suspicious

Email careeros.info@gmail.com with the link to the listing. Employers and programme providers can also ask us to correct or remove a listing this way.

Recruitment scams

Noetra Careers never asks for money to apply, and real employers don't either. If anyone asks you to pay a fee, send money or share bank details for a job or scholarship, don't pay, and report it to us.

Privacy and your data

See the Privacy Policy. You can delete your account in the app or on the account deletion page.

Updates

New jobs, scholarships and deadlines are posted on our WhatsApp channel and on X at @NoetraCareers. Both are for announcements only, so please use email or the app for questions.

Campus partners

Student groups and universities can book a free career session or become a campus ambassador.
