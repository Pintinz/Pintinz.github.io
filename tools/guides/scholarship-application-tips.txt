Fully funded scholarships are competitive, but every year many students from Nigeria and across Africa win them. Strong applications have one thing in common: they start early and follow the instructions exactly.

## 1. Start 9 to 12 months early

Many scholarships for study starting in September close between October and March, and some require you to have applied to, or been admitted by, a university first. Make a list of target scholarships with their deadlines and requirements.

## 2. Check eligibility carefully

Before you spend time on an application, confirm:

- **Nationality and residence**: some scholarships are only for citizens of specific countries.
- **Level of study**: undergraduate, master's or PhD.
- **Academic record**: minimum degree class or grade point average.
- **Work experience**: many master's scholarships ask for two or more years.
- **Commitments**: some require you to return home after your studies.

If you don't meet a requirement, move on to the next scholarship.

## 3. Prepare your documents

Common requirements include:

- Academic transcripts and degree certificate
- A valid international passport
- Two or three reference letters
- A personal statement or study objectives essay
- A CV
- Proof of English language ability, if required
- A university admission letter, if required

Request transcripts and references early, as they often take weeks.

## 4. Write a strong personal statement

- Answer the exact questions asked, in the order asked, within the word limit.
- Be specific about your goals: what you want to study, why that course, and how you'll use it afterwards.
- Show evidence of leadership, impact or community involvement with real examples.
- Explain how you fit the provider's aims. Read their mission and use it as a guide.
- Write in your own words and get feedback from someone you trust.

## 5. Avoid scams

Genuine scholarships never ask you to pay a fee to receive the award. Always apply through the official scholarship or university website, and check the web address carefully.

## 6. After you apply

- Keep a copy of everything you submit.
- Prepare for interviews, as many scholarships interview shortlisted candidates.
- Keep applying to other options while you wait.
