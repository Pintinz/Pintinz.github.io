Many medium and large employers receive hundreds of applications per role and use an applicant tracking system (ATS) to collect and sort them. A CV that the system can't read properly, or that doesn't match the job, may never reach a recruiter.

## Formatting that ATS can read

- Use a simple, single-column layout. Tables, text boxes and columns can scramble the order of your text.
- Use standard section headings: *Profile*, *Experience*, *Education*, *Skills*, *Certifications*.
- Use a common font such as Arial or Calibri, 10 to 12 points.
- Don't put important information in headers, footers or images.
- Save as PDF unless the employer asks for Word, and check that the PDF's text can be selected and copied.

## Match the job description

ATS and recruiters both look for evidence that you match the role. For each application:

1. Highlight the skills, tools and qualifications in the job description.
2. Use the same words in your CV where they truthfully describe you. If the advert says "financial modelling", don't only write "Excel analysis".
3. Put the most relevant experience first in each section.

Never add skills you don't have. Interviews and tests will check them.

## Write achievements, not duties

Compare these two lines:

- *Responsible for social media accounts.*
- *Grew the society's Instagram from 400 to 2,100 followers in 6 months by planning a weekly content calendar.*

The second shows what you did and what it achieved. Start bullet points with an action verb (led, built, improved, organised, reduced) and add a number wherever you can.

## Keep it focused

- **Length:** one page for most graduates, two pages at most.
- **Profile:** three or four lines on who you are, what you're good at and what role you want.
- **Contact details:** phone number, professional email, city and LinkedIn. Leave out your full home address, date of birth and photo unless the employer or country specifically asks for them.

## Check before you send

- Proofread twice and ask someone else to read it.
- Make sure dates are consistent and there are no unexplained gaps.
- Name the file clearly, for example *Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf*.
