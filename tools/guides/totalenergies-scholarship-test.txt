The NNPC Limited / TotalEnergies National Merit Scholarship Scheme (NMSS) supports first- and second-year undergraduates in any course with a yearly grant from the year of award until graduation. Applications for the 2026/2027 session are open from **10 November to 7 December 2026**, and shortlisted candidates then sit a selection test. Here's what's officially known and how to get ready.

> CareerOS is not affiliated with NNPC Limited or TotalEnergies. Facts about the scheme below come from the official TotalEnergies scholarship portal. Confirm details there before you apply.

## Who can apply

According to the official portal, you must:

- be a Nigerian student registered for full-time undergraduate study at an NUC-accredited Nigerian university;
- be a certified **100-level or 200-level** student when you apply;
- have a UTME score of at least **200**;
- if you're in 200 level, have a CGPA of at least **2.5 on a 5.0 scale**.

You can't apply if you're in 300 level or above, or if you already hold a similar award from a company in the oil and gas industry. Any course of study is accepted.

Applications are made online through the TotalEnergies scholarship website only; don't send applications by email. The application is **completely free**: TotalEnergies says it doesn't ask for payment or accredit agents to act for it.

## How selection works

The official portal says:

1. Applications are online only, through the scholarship website.
2. **Only shortlisted candidates are invited to selection tests.**
3. TotalEnergies may change the schedule or the selection test process.

So after you apply, keep checking the email address and account you applied with. Because the schedule can change, prepare early rather than waiting for the invitation.

## What the test is likely to cover

TotalEnergies doesn't publish a syllabus for the NMSS test. Undergraduate scholarship tests in Nigeria commonly include:

- **Numerical reasoning**: percentages, fractions, ratios, simple interest and word problems.
- **Verbal reasoning**: vocabulary, comprehension and choosing the correct word or meaning.
- **Logical reasoning**: number and letter sequences and odd-one-out questions.

Use this as a practical plan, not a promise about the exact format. If your invitation names the sections, focus on those.

## Practice questions

These are original CareerOS practice questions written in the style of an undergraduate scholarship test. They are **not** past questions from the real NMSS test.

**1. Percentages.** What is 25% of 360?

*Answer: 90.* 25% is a quarter, and 360 ÷ 4 = 90.

**2. Simple interest.** ₦50,000 is saved at 10% simple interest per year. How much interest is earned after 3 years?

*Answer: ₦15,000.* One year's interest is 10% of ₦50,000 = ₦5,000, and 3 × ₦5,000 = ₦15,000.

**3. Proportion.** If 4 pens cost ₦600, how much do 10 pens cost?

*Answer: ₦1,500.* One pen costs ₦600 ÷ 4 = ₦150, and 10 × ₦150 = ₦1,500.

**4. Sequences.** What comes next: 3, 9, 27, 81, ?

*Answer: 243.* Each number is three times the one before it.

**5. Odd one out.** Lagos, Kano, Ghana, Ibadan, Enugu.

*Answer: Ghana.* It's a country; the others are Nigerian cities.

**6. Vocabulary.** Choose the word closest in meaning to **meticulous**: (a) careless (b) careful (c) quick (d) noisy.

*Answer: (b) careful.* Meticulous means paying close attention to every detail.

**7. Comprehension.** *"The scheme supports first- and second-year undergraduates irrespective of course of study."* Which statement is supported by the sentence?

- (a) Only science students can apply.
- (b) Students in any course can apply.
- (c) Final-year students can apply.

*Answer: (b).* "Irrespective of course of study" means the course doesn't matter.

## A two-week preparation plan

- **Days 1–2:** take a short timed test in each area and note where you lose the most marks.
- **Days 3–6:** rebuild the basics in your weakest area, such as percentages, fractions or vocabulary, then do short timed drills.
- **Days 7–10:** mix all areas in timed sets of 20–30 questions.
- **Days 11–13:** take full mock tests under exam conditions and review every wrong answer.
- **Day 14:** rest, re-read the invitation and get your documents ready.

## On the day

- Check the invitation for the date, time, venue or link, and what you must bring.
- Arrive or log in early so a slow start doesn't cost you time.
- Answer the questions you're sure of first, then return to the hard ones if the test lets you.
- Don't spend too long on one question; keep moving and come back if you have time.

## Frequently asked questions

### When does the 2026/2027 TotalEnergies NMSS application close?

The official portal lists **7 December 2026** as the closing date. Applications opened on 10 November 2026.

### Is the TotalEnergies scholarship free to apply for?

Yes. The official portal says scholarship applications are completely free and that TotalEnergies doesn't request payment or accredit agents.

### Can 300-level students apply?

No. The NMSS is for certified 100-level and 200-level students. Students in 300 level or above should not apply.

### What UTME score do I need for the TotalEnergies scholarship?

At least 200. If you're in 200 level, you also need a CGPA of at least 2.5 on a 5.0 scale.

### Are there official TotalEnergies NMSS past questions?

TotalEnergies doesn't publish past questions. Be wary of anyone selling "leaked" questions; practising the common test areas is the reliable way to prepare.
