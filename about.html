About Noetra Careers

Noetra Careers is a free Android app that helps you find jobs, graduate programmes, internships and scholarships, prepare for the tests and interviews that come with them, and keep track of every application in one place.

What you can do

  • Find opportunities. Jobs, jobs in Nigeria, graduate programmes, internships and scholarships, taken from official company and programme pages.
  • Research employers. Company pages show who is hiring now and what they are hiring for.
  • Practise aptitude tests. Numerical, verbal and logical questions, timed or at your own pace, with worked answers.
  • Prepare for interviews. Question banks, mock interviews, STAR answer coaching and an AI interviewer.
  • Check your CV. See how your CV reads to applicant tracking systems for a specific role, and what to improve.
  • Track applications. Stages, interviews and deadlines in one place, with reminders.
  • Learn from others. A community feed and company, industry and role communities where members share updates, milestones and questions.

Where listings come from

Every job and scholarship links to the employer's or provider's own page, and you always apply there. Noetra Careers is not the employer or scholarship provider for the listings it shows, and never charges you to apply. If a listing looks wrong or has closed, tell us.
