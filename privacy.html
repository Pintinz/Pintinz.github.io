Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

In short: we collect what you give us to run CareerOS and what we need to keep it safe. We do not sell your personal data. Email connection, the AI interviewer and personalised ads are optional, and you can delete your account at any time from the app.

1. Who we are

CareerOS ("CareerOS", "we", "us") is a careers app published on Google Play by AutotecHUB. We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at careeros.info@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Your Career Passport: profile details you choose to add, such as education, work experience, skills, certifications, projects, target roles, industries, preferred locations, work mode and whether you are open to relocation.
  • CVs and documents: when you upload a CV for analysis, we extract and keep its text to run the check; we do not keep the original file. Other documents you upload are stored privately and are available only to you.
  • Applications you track: companies, roles, stages, interview dates, notes and reminders.
  • Interview practice: your answers, scores and STAR stories. For the AI Interview Coach, see section 4.
  • Community activity: posts, replies, reactions, messages and communities you join, and the public profile you choose to show.
  • Support messages: anything you send us when you ask for help.

Information collected when you use CareerOS

  • Device and app information: device type, operating system, app version and a notification token so we can send you push notifications.
  • Usage and security logs: sign-ins, requests to our servers and errors, used to run, secure and improve the service.
  • Advertising identifier: used by Google AdMob to show ads (see section 6).
  • Purchases: when you buy credits, Google Play processes the payment. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, which we store in protected form. We never receive your card or bank details.

Optional: connected email

If you choose to connect Gmail or Outlook, CareerOS requests read-only access to find recruitment-related messages and suggest updates to applications you already track. We do not use your email for advertising or for any other purpose. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and you can separately delete the recruitment email data we hold.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide CareerOS features.
  • To show opportunities, career insights and preparation tools relevant to the target roles, experience and locations in your Career Passport. We explain why something is recommended, and we only use what you have told us.
  • To send notifications and emails you have asked for, such as interview reminders, application updates and receipts.
  • To process credit purchases, transfers and refunds, and to prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To keep CareerOS secure, investigate problems and improve the service.
  • To comply with the law.

We process your data to provide the service you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests in running and securing CareerOS, with your consent where required (for example connected email and personalised ads), and to meet legal obligations.

4. AI features

The AI Interview Coach uses OpenAI to run a spoken interview. While a session is running, your voice and the interview context (the role you are practising for and relevant parts of your Career Passport) are sent to OpenAI to generate the interviewer's questions and responses. We keep the transcript, scores and feedback so you can review your progress. Audio recordings stay on your device, and you can delete them there.

CV checks, job matching and recommendations are calculated by CareerOS itself and are not sent to outside AI services.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run CareerOS, under their own privacy and security commitments:

Other CareerOS users see only what you choose to make public, such as your community profile, posts and replies. Employers do not see your Career Passport, CV or interview practice through CareerOS. When you apply for a job, you apply on the employer's own site, and their privacy policy applies there.

We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of our users or others, or as part of a business transfer, in which case this policy continues to apply.

6. Advertising

CareerOS shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads are always labelled "Advertisement" and are never shown during tests, interviews or sign-in. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) we ask for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account in the app, we delete your profile, Career Passport, documents and application history. We keep limited records, such as purchase and transaction records, and security logs, only for as long as we need them for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention or security reasons.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Your documents are private to your account and are only served to you through short-lived secure links. Passwords are stored as hashes, and access to our systems is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if something goes wrong.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under Nigeria's Data Protection Act 2023, the GDPR or similar laws, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data (you can edit most of it directly in the app);
  • delete your data (you can delete your account in the app);
  • object to or restrict certain processing;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by disconnecting email or changing ad settings;
  • complain to your data protection authority.

To make a request, email careeros.info@gmail.com. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in other countries. When they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards so your data stays protected.

11. Children

CareerOS is intended for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as CareerOS changes. We will change the date at the top, and tell you in the app or by email if the changes are significant.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: careeros.info@gmail.com.
